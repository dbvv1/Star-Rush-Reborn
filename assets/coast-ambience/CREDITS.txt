  Actual load 46 s; peak allocated 9.895 GiB / peak reserved 12.803 GiB (bytes / 2**30).
  The torch.cuda.max_memory_* counters were not reset between seeds, so these peaks are
  process-cumulative, not independent per-seed peaks. No OOM, but this is not evidence of

  Generation: seed 0 took 446.54 s, seed 1 took 153.2 s, including device-to-host copy and
  WAV writing. The first seed was slower than the second; the reason is unknown.

  Local inference adaptations, needed to run the pinned source with the published weights:
  - The pinned source's DAC.load() expects a numpy codec archive, but the published
    vae_128d_48k.pth is a torch state_dict. The runner constructs DAC from the checkpoint
    metadata and loads the state_dict directly.
  - from_pretrained() moves models without casting them. The runner casts real parameters
    and buffers to bfloat16 while leaving complex rotary buffers in complex64.
  These are runner-side changes only; no model weights were modified or retrained.
